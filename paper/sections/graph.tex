\section{Representing the cluster and its traffic}
\label{sec:graph}

\paragraph{One graph, normalised.}
The cluster becomes a directed graph whose vertices are accelerators, network
interfaces, CPU sockets and switches, and whose edges are links, every capacity
in bytes per second whatever unit the source used. A resource that several
links contend for---a host bridge, an uplink, a switch port---is its own
vertex, with a capacity and the share already reserved outside this
deployment, so that a cut computation sees it: the capacity between two device
sets is a maximum flow in which the contended element is a real node, with the
reservation subtracted before any bound is computed.

\paragraph{Demand.}
A candidate's traffic is a set of flows, each with its participants, its bytes
per event, its events per request, and whether a latency target charges for it.
The last field is load-bearing: ingress and egress consume an uplink's capacity
though no SLO charges for their time, and a model that priced only charged
flows would call a saturated uplink idle.

% claim: C17
\paragraph{A link does not have \emph{a} bandwidth.}
Measured on the node of Section~\ref{sec:eval}, one PCIe path between two
accelerators sustains \egfourPeerBridge~GB/s for a direct peer copy and
\egfourBusbwTwo~GB/s of bus bandwidth for a two-rank all-reduce across it; a
tensor-parallel group of four, on two NVLink pairs joined by that bridge, runs
its all-reduce at \egfourBusbwFour~GB/s. The two all-reduces, both bound by the
bridge, differ by more than a factor of two. A measurement is therefore keyed
by the collective, the number of ranks, the message-size band and the process
binding, and a figure recorded under one key is never substituted for another.

\paragraph{Which resource is shared is settled by measurement.}
The representation can express that two links contend; it cannot decide
whether they do. Section~\ref{sec:eval} reports a declaration the measurement
contradicted, so a shared-resource vertex is treated as a hypothesis about the
machine until something has put a probe on the wire.
